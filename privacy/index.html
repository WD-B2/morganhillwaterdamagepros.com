Privacy Policy

Plain language about what we collect when you call or visit this site.

Call (844) 950-6900

Information from phone calls

When you call (844) 950-6900, we ask for your name, address and a description of the problem so we can send a crew. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

We use call information to dispatch crews, document jobs and improve service. We do not sell your call information.

Information from this website

This website has no forms and does not ask you to type personal information. Our web host and standard server tools may record technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited. This data helps keep the site running and secure.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it to reply.

Third-party services

Pages may load fonts from Google Fonts and maps from Google Maps. Those providers receive technical data, such as your IP address, under their own policies. We do not control those policies.

Sharing and your choices

We share information with crews, insurers or contractors only as needed to do the job you asked for, or when the law requires it. You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by email.

California residents may have additional rights under state law. Contact us at contact@morganhillwaterdamagepros.com to make a request.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy. The date at the bottom of the page shows the last update. Questions go to contact@morganhillwaterdamagepros.com or to 18585 Monterey Road, Morgan Hill, CA 95037.
