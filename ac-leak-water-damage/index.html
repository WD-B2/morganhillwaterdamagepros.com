Why air conditioners leak

A running air conditioner pulls moisture out of the air, which collects on the coil and drains away through a condensate line. That line can clog with algae and dust. When it does, water backs up into the pan and spills.

Other causes include a cracked drain pan, a frozen evaporator coil that thaws, and a poorly sloped drain line. Industry guidelines for water loss work, such as ANSI/IICRC S500, treat a slow condensate leak as clean water that degrades over time. Air handlers in attics and closets are the usual trouble spots, because a leak there reaches finished ceilings.

What to do while the unit is leaking

Switch off the air conditioner and place a bucket under the drip. Do not touch wet fixtures, and call a utility if water reaches wiring.

  • Switch off the unit at the thermostat and the breaker.
  • Move stored items out of the wet closet or attic access.
  • Photograph the stain, the drain pan and the air handler.

Signs your AC leak has already done damage

Any of these deserves a look with a moisture meter. A stain may be the tip of a much larger wet area.

  • A tan or brown ring on the ceiling below the air handler.
  • A musty smell near the closet, attic hatch or return.
  • Soft, bubbling or stained drywall at the edge of a utility closet.
  • Wet insulation visible around the drain pan.
  • Rising humidity in a room near the unit.

What an AC leak repair looks like

We map moisture across the ceiling, the walls and the area around the air handler. Wet insulation is removed and bagged, and soaked drywall is cut out. Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the framing.

An HVAC technician clears or replaces the drain line and checks the coil. That repair prevents a repeat, so we coordinate with your technician. When the ceiling is open, our ceiling water damage repair crew can close it and match the texture.

Mold risk after a slow AC leak

A slow leak keeps materials damp for weeks, which is the condition mold needs. If you smell mustiness or see dark spots on the ceiling, call us before painting over it. Our mold remediation page explains how growth is handled.

We do not make health claims about mold. If you have symptoms you believe are related, talk to your physician.

Keeping the drain line clear

Have a technician flush the condensate line each year before cooling season. Install a float switch that shuts the unit off when the pan fills. Check the pan under attic units each spring.
