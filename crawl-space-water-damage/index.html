Crawl Space Water Damage Repair in Morgan Hill, CA

Water under the house soaks insulation, joists and subfloors out of sight. We pump it out, dry the framing and check for mold growth.

Call (844) 950-6900All services

Crawl space water damage in Morgan Hill often goes unseen until floors cup or a musty smell reaches the living space. Water collects from a leaking line, a poor drainage slope or runoff that finds its way under the house. Call (844) 950-6900 and we will schedule a crew to inspect and dry the space.

If you can safely look, check for standing water, wet insulation hanging down, or damp soil. Do not enter a crawl space with water near wiring or with any sewage smell.

Where crawl space water comes from

A leaking supply or drain line is a common source, since pipes run under the floor in raised foundations. Poor grading around the foundation sends rain toward the vents. Heavy winter storms can raise groundwater enough to seep through.

We have not verified how many Morgan Hill homes have crawl spaces, so we do not give a share. If your home sits on a raised foundation, the space under it deserves a yearly look.

Signs of water under the house

Any of these suggests a moisture check is worth ordering before the subfloor suffers.

  • Floors that feel soft, cupped or uneven in rooms above.
  • A musty or earthy smell from vents or floor registers.
  • Insulation sagging or lying on the soil.
  • Visible rust on metal ducts or fasteners.
  • Pest activity or white mineral deposits on foundation walls.

How a wet crawl space is dried

Crews pump out standing water, remove wet insulation and set dehumidifiers and air movers sized to the space. A vapor barrier on the soil, where one is missing or torn, helps keep moisture from returning.

Joists and subfloor are checked with a meter. Framing that dries to normal levels stays. Soft or rotted wood is flagged for carpentry repair. Our structural drying page covers the equipment, and a leaking pipe above may also need burst pipe water damage work.

Mold and rot in wet crawl spaces

Damp wood is where mold starts. Floor joists, subfloor panels and sill plates can hold moisture long after the standing water is gone. If growth is present, containment and removal follow the ANSI/IICRC S520 standard. See our mold remediation page for that process.

We do not make health claims. If you believe mold is affecting your health, talk with your physician.

Crawl space cleanup cost factors

Access matters as much as water depth. These are the main price drivers.

  • Height and access of the crawl space, which affects labor time.
  • Volume of water and whether pumping is needed.
  • Amount of insulation and debris to remove.
  • Drying days and equipment count.
  • Whether mold treatment or a vapor barrier is added.

Homes on slopes near Paradise Valley and the foothills can collect runoff against the uphill foundation wall. Check that downspouts discharge well away from the house and that soil slopes away from the foundation.
