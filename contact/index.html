What to say when you call

Tell dispatch where the water is and where it came from. Mention whether the power is on, whether sewage is involved and how many rooms are wet.

Give the address and a cross street. If the water is still flowing, close the main valve first, then call.

Before you dial

  • Stop the water source if it is safe to do so.
  • Stay out of rooms where water touches outlets or the electric panel.
  • Lift small items off wet floors.
  • Take a few photos of the water and the damage.
  • Have your insurer's phone number nearby.
