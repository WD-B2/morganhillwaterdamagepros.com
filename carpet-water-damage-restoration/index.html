Carpet Water Damage Restoration in Morgan Hill, CA

Wet carpet can often be saved if the water is clean and drying starts fast. We tell you honestly when it cannot.

Call (844) 950-6900All services

Carpet water damage in Morgan Hill is most often caused by a leak, an overflow or a storm. Carpet holds water in its backing and pad, and the subfloor can stay wet long after the surface feels dry. Call (844) 950-6900 and we will schedule a crew to extract and dry the carpet.

If the carpet is soaked, remove furniture and lift wet items. Blot standing water, and avoid walking on the wet area. Do not turn on a regular household vacuum.

How long before wet carpet becomes a problem

Carpet that stays wet for more than a day or two can develop odor, and mold can start in the pad and backing. Clean water gives a longer window than dirty water. A soaked carpet that sat for several days from a slow leak is a much harder case.

Many carpets are saved when extraction and drying begin quickly. If the water was dirty, the carpet is usually removed, which is explained on our sewage backup cleanup page.

Wet carpet decision guide

These are the usual calls we make on site.

  • Clean water, caught within a day: extract, dry in place, often save the carpet.
  • Clean water, pad soaked: lift carpet, replace pad, dry subfloor, reinstall carpet.
  • Gray water (washer overflow): treat, often discard pad.
  • Sewage or flood water: remove and discard carpet and pad.
  • Water standing for days: expect mold checks and likely replacement.

How carpet is extracted and dried

A weighted extractor pulls water out of the carpet and pad. The technician then lifts a corner and checks the subfloor with a meter. Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the carpet while it is lifted or floated on special mats.

Pad that is soaked is almost always replaced because it holds water and dries slowly. The carpet may be reinstalled on new pad if it is clean and the backing is intact. See water extraction for the earlier stage of this work.

Mold in carpet from water damage

A carpet that smells musty after drying may still hold moisture in the pad or subfloor. If growth is visible on the back of the carpet or on the subfloor, our mold remediation team follows ANSI/IICRC S520.

We do not make health claims. If someone in your home is concerned about symptoms, a physician is the right person to ask.

What you can do while you wait

Lift furniture off the wet carpet and place foil or plastic under the legs. Remove loose items, rugs and boxes. Blot standing water with clean towels, working from the edges toward the center.

Open closet doors and move clothing off the floor. Keep children and pets off the wet area. Do not use heat guns or a household hair dryer on the carpet, since that dries the surface and leaves the pad wet.

Take photos and cut a small sample from a closet corner for your records. The sample helps match the carpet if part of it has to be replaced.
