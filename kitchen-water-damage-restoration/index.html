Kitchen Water Damage Restoration in Morgan Hill, CA

Leaks under the sink and behind the dishwasher soak cabinets and floors out of sight. We dry the space and rebuild what cannot be saved.

Call (844) 950-6900All services

Kitchen water damage in Morgan Hill often starts under a sink or behind a dishwasher. Small leaks swell cabinet bases, loosen flooring and feed mold in the dark. Call (844) 950-6900 and we will schedule a crew to check and dry the space.

If water is actively leaking, close the valve under the sink or the main. Empty the cabinet and photograph the damage before you touch anything.

Where kitchen leaks start

The sink drain trap, the faucet supply lines and the dishwasher connection leak most. Refrigerator ice-maker lines and garbage disposals add to the list. A leak that drips once a day can stay hidden for months.

Cabinet bases made of particleboard swell and crumble when wet. Plywood fares better, and solid wood can often be dried. The material decides whether cabinets are saved.

Signs of kitchen water damage

If you see any of these, a moisture reading can tell you whether the damage is old or ongoing.

  • Swollen or peeling cabinet base and toe-kick.
  • A soft or bouncy floor near the sink or dishwasher.
  • Musty smell when you open the under-sink cabinet.
  • Stains on the wall behind the sink.
  • Water spots on the ceiling in the room below.

Drying cabinets and floors

We remove the toe-kick and open the cabinet base to let air reach the wet area. Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the cabinet, the wall and the subfloor, and injection drying ports can reach under hard flooring. Drying follows ANSI/IICRC S500 guidelines, which call for documented moisture readings before a cabinet is closed up again.

Swollen particleboard is usually replaced. Solid cabinet frames often dry in place. Flooring beneath is judged by its type, and our hardwood floor water damage page explains how wood reacts.

What to do before the crew arrives

Empty the cabinet under the sink and set the contents on a dry surface. Cardboard, sponges and cleaning products soaked in a leak are usually discarded, while plastic bottles can be wiped and kept.

Photograph the cabinet base, the pipes and the floor from several angles. If a pipe fitting is dripping, place a small bowl under it and note how fast it fills. That detail helps the plumber and the adjuster judge how long the leak has been running.

Leave the cabinet doors open so air can reach the wet surfaces. A fan pointed into the cabinet helps a little, but it is no replacement for measured drying.

Kitchen restoration cost factors

Cabinet replacement drives kitchen costs more than any other single item.

  • Cabinet material and how many units are wet.
  • Type of flooring and whether it can dry in place.
  • Drywall cut and any mold present.
  • Equipment days.
  • Rebuild scope including countertops and plumbing reconnection.
