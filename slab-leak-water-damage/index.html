Emergency Slab Leak Water Damage in Morgan Hill, CA

A pipe leaking under the concrete floor can soak flooring and walls for weeks. We handle the water damage while a plumber finds the break.

A slab leak in Morgan Hill can turn urgent without a sound. When a pipe under the concrete floor breaks, water rises through cracks and into flooring, carpet and the base of walls. Our 24/7 dispatch line takes the call, whether the water is visible or you only suspect a hidden leak.

If you see water coming up through the floor, close the main valve. If the floor feels warm in one spot, switch off the water heater and call a plumber to test. Move rugs and furniture off wet areas.

We dry the floor and walls once the leak is stopped. A plumber locates and repairs the pipe, which often means breaking into the slab.

Is water running under your floor?

Most people search for a slab leak using what they notice, not the trade name. They notice a sound of running water when every tap is off, a warm patch on the floor, or a water bill that jumped. They notice a damp carpet, a musty smell, or a cracked tile that was fine last month.

Those are all symptoms of the same problem, a leak in a supply line beneath the concrete. A slab leak is a leak in a pipe embedded in or beneath the foundation slab, and it can be a hot water line or a cold one.

Slab leak emergency: what to do first

Do these while you wait for a plumber and for our crew.

  1. Watch the meter. Turn off every tap and appliance, then check whether the meter dial still moves.
  2. Close the main valve. This stops the leak while you arrange repairs.
  3. Check for warmth. A hot spot on the floor points to a hot water line under the slab.
  4. Lift and protect. Move rugs, boxes and furniture off the wet floor.
  5. Call a plumber and our line. A leak detection test pinpoints the break, and we plan the drying.

What slab leaks do to flooring and walls

Water under a slab finds its way up through cracks and joints at the edges. It wicks into baseboards and the bottom of drywall, then spreads into carpet and flooring. Wood and laminate swell, and tile can lift where grout is weak.

Water that has been there for weeks may be Category 2 or worse. The ANSI/IICRC S500 standard explains why time moves water up a category. Mold can start in carpet pad and the bottom of walls, and our carpet water damage and hardwood floor water damage pages explain how those floors are judged.

How a slab leak is found and fixed

Plumbers use acoustic listening equipment, thermal imaging and pressure tests to find the break without tearing up the entire floor. Repairs may mean breaking into the slab and patching the pipe. In some homes, a new line is run overhead instead.

We coordinate with the plumber so drying starts before the slab is closed. Moisture readings in the surrounding structure tell us when it is safe to patch and finish. The overall method is covered on our structural drying page.

Slab leak water damage cost factors

The plumbing repair and the water damage work are priced separately. For the water damage side, these factors matter.

  • How long the water has been rising before it was found.
  • Type of flooring and whether it can dry in place.
  • Amount of wall base and drywall that is wet.
  • Whether the water was a hot or cold line, and any mold present.
  • Drying days and the rebuild scope.
