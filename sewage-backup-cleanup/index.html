Emergency Sewage Backup Cleanup in Morgan Hill, CA

Sewage carries bacteria and should never be handled bare-handed. Keep everyone out, stop using water and call our 24/7 line.

A sewage backup in Morgan Hill is an emergency and a health risk. Wastewater carries bacteria, viruses and parasites, and it should be handled only with protective gear. Our dispatch line answers 24/7, so call at any hour and keep everyone out of the affected rooms.

Stop using sinks, toilets, showers and the washing machine, because every flush sends more water into the line. Keep children and pets away, and do not touch the water without gloves and boots. If the water is near outlets, stay out and call the utility or 911.

Sewage cleanup is its own discipline. Our crews wear protective suits, remove contaminated materials and disinfect what stays, following the contamination rules in ANSI/IICRC S500.

Dirty water coming up from a drain: what it means

People rarely search for the trade term. They search for what they see, a toilet that overflows when the washer drains, a floor drain gurgling, or brown water in the tub. Those are all signs that wastewater is backing up into the home.

In the industry this is Category 3 water, the most contaminated class. It can come from a blocked house line, a clog in the street main, or a failed septic system. Each source needs a different response.

Sewage emergency: what to do first

Protect your household before you protect your belongings.

  1. Stop using water. No flushing, laundry or dishwasher until the line is clear.
  2. Keep people out. Children, pets and anyone with a weak immune system should leave the area.
  3. Do not enter power-hazard areas. If water touches outlets or the panel, call the utility or 911 first.
  4. Open windows if safe. Ventilate, but do not run fans that spread contamination to clean rooms.
  5. Call a plumber and our line. The plumber clears the blockage, and we handle the contamination.

Is the problem on your side or the street's?

Most clogs sit in the private line between the house and the street. A plumber with a camera can see whether the blockage is yours. If the backup comes from the city main, your water utility should be notified.

The City of Morgan Hill's utilities corporation yard at 100 Edes Court lists weekday hours and no 24-hour number, so at night a plumber is the faster way to learn which side the blockage is on. In San Martin and other unincorporated areas, many homes use septic systems rather than city sewer, which changes who responds. See our San Martin page for that context.

How sewage cleanup is done

Crews extract the contaminated water first, then remove porous materials that cannot be cleaned, such as carpet, pad, drywall and insulation. Hard surfaces are cleaned and treated with an antimicrobial agent. Air movers and dehumidifiers dry what remains.

Items that touched sewage are usually discarded if porous. Our water extraction team handles the bulk removal, and structural drying follows once the area is cleaned.

What drives sewage cleanup cost

Sewage jobs cost more than clean-water jobs because of protective equipment and disposal.

  • Square footage reached and depth of the contaminated water.
  • Amount of carpet, pad and drywall that must be removed.
  • Protective equipment and disposal requirements.
  • Drying days and antimicrobial treatment.
  • Rebuild scope for flooring, base and paint.
