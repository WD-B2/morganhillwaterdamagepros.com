Rain finds every weak spot in a roof, window or door. Call our 24/7 line during or after a storm and we will start the water removal.

Storm water damage in Morgan Hill is an emergency while rain is still falling. Water enters through roofs, windows, doors and low openings, and it keeps coming until the storm passes or the opening is covered. Our dispatch line answers 24/7, so call at any hour, including during storms.

Move people and valuables away from wet areas and place buckets under drips. Cover broken windows if you can do so safely. A roofer should tarp the opening, since roof work in the rain is dangerous.

We handle the interior water damage. That means removing standing water, drying wet insulation and drywall, and checking for moisture where it hides.

Storm leak emergency: what to do while it is still raining

Protect people first, then property.

  1. Stay off wet roofs and ladders. Roof work in the rain causes serious falls.
  2. Contain interior water. Use buckets, towels and plastic sheeting under leaks.
  3. Cut power to wet areas if the panel is dry. Wet ceilings and fixtures are an electrical risk.
  4. Photograph the damage. Capture exterior and interior shots for the claim.
  5. Call dispatch. Tell us which rooms are wet and whether outside water is entering.

How storms have hit Morgan Hill

Valley Water recorded heavy rain flooding in town in January 2017. A few weeks later Anderson Reservoir spilled for the first time since 2006, a reminder of how quickly a wet winter escalates.

Those events were creek and reservoir driven, but even ordinary storms test roofs, gutters and grading. A home that handled a light rain can still fail in a heavy one, so a check of flashing and downspouts before winter pays off.

Where storm water gets in

Roof flashing, worn shingles, skylights and chimney collars are common entry points. Windows leak at the sill when caulk fails. Doors let water in at the threshold, especially at garages and patios.

Water can also enter from below when gutters overflow against the foundation. In those cases, see flood damage cleanup and crawl space water damage for the lower-level effects.

Cleanup after the rain stops

Crews extract standing water, remove wet insulation and cut out soaked drywall. Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the framing, and moisture readings tell us when to stop.

When the ceiling is stained or sagging, our ceiling water damage repair team handles the rebuild. A roofer completes the permanent roof repair.

What to document after a storm

Photograph the roof, gutters and windows from the ground as well as the interior stains. Note the date and time the storm hit and when you first saw water inside.

Keep wet materials that were removed until the adjuster says otherwise. If you must discard something for safety, photograph it first. Save receipts for tarps, buckets and any temporary repairs, since reasonable emergency costs are part of a typical claim.

What affects storm damage cleanup cost

Storm losses vary with how long water was entering.

  • Duration of the leak and amount of water that entered.
  • Whether insulation and ceiling drywall are involved.
  • Floor type and how deeply it was wetted.
  • Drying days and equipment count.
  • Rebuild scope including paint and texture.
