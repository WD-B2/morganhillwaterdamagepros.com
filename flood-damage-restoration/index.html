Emergency Flood Damage Cleanup in Morgan Hill, CA

Floodwater is contaminated and moves fast. Call the 24/7 line before you touch anything, and wait for guidance on what is safe to handle.

Call (844) 950-6900All services

Flood damage in Morgan Hill is an emergency, and it is also a health matter. Water from creeks and streets carries sewage, soil and chemicals into the house. Our dispatch line is open 24/7, so call at any hour once the water starts to recede or if you cannot tell whether the house is safe.

Do not wade through floodwater if power is on. Leave the house if water is above outlets, and call 911 for gas or electrical hazards before you call us. Photograph the waterline from a dry spot, and keep boots and gloves on if you must enter.

Flood cleanup is different from a pipe loss. Floodwater is treated as Category 3 water, which means contaminated, and many porous materials have to be removed rather than dried.

Flood emergency: what to do first

Safety comes before cleanup. These steps protect you and your claim.

  1. Get everyone out. Move people and pets to higher ground before water rises further.
  2. Shut off power at the panel only if it is dry. Otherwise call the utility from outside.
  3. Photograph the waterline. Take wide and close shots, then list damaged items.
  4. Do not use household vacs or fans. They can spread contaminated water and create electrical hazards.
  5. Call dispatch. Tell us the depth, how long it sat and whether sewage is mixed in.

How Morgan Hill floods happen

Per the City of Morgan Hill, about one-twelfth of town is a FEMA high-risk flood zone. Nine named creeks matter here, namely Llagas, West Little Llagas, Edmundson, Foothill, Paradise, Tennant, Coyote, Maple and Corralitos.

Valley Water reports that Llagas Creek has flooded Morgan Hill six times since 2000. The February 2017 Anderson Reservoir spill, the first since 2006, showed how fast a wet winter can send creek water into streets.

If you want to know whether your address sits in a mapped zone, check the FEMA flood map for your parcel. A lot outside the mapped zone can still take water from a storm drain or a blocked culvert.

What the Upper Llagas project means for homeowners

The final phase of the Upper Llagas Creek project runs through 2027, and Valley Water expects it to protect about 1,100 homes and 500 businesses. The work includes a box culvert at Main and Hale Avenues and culverts below Edes Street.

That is future protection, not a reason to skip precautions now. Know where sandbags are handed out before the rain starts.

How floodwater is removed and the home is cleaned

Crews pump out the water, then remove materials that cannot be cleaned, such as soaked carpet, pad and wet insulation. Drywall is cut above the waterline when contamination wicked upward. Surfaces that stay are cleaned with disinfecting agents and dried with air movers and dehumidifiers.

Hard surfaces such as concrete, tile and sealed wood can often be cleaned and kept. Cabinets and trim are judged one by one. Our water extraction team handles the pumping, and basement water damage cleanup covers lower-level flooding.

Flood insurance: what homeowners often miss

A flood loss needs its own policy, since standard coverage excludes it. NFIP policies usually start 30 days after purchase, so buying one once a storm is forecast is too late. The city's Community Rating System status, held since 2003, trims premiums by 15 percent for eligible policyholders.

If you have a flood policy, call your carrier right away and keep damaged items until the adjuster has inspected them. If you do not, FEMA assistance may be available after a federal declaration. We do not promise coverage, since your policy governs.

What drives flood cleanup cost

Flood jobs vary widely because of how many materials come out.

  • Depth of the water and how long it stayed.
  • Square footage affected, plus how many rooms.
  • Amount of porous material removed and disposed of.
  • Days of drying equipment and disinfecting steps.
  • Rebuild scope for drywall, flooring, cabinets and trim.
