Water Damage Restoration Coyote, CA Near Morgan Hill

Coyote Creek runs through this community, and winter storms test it. We answer 24/7 and send a crew from Morgan Hill when water is rising or spreading.

Coyote is an unincorporated community along Coyote Creek between San Jose and the Madrone district of Morgan Hill. Part of it lies inside San Jose city limits. US-101 was rerouted about 0.6 mile to the east, which left older roads and homes near the creek on their own lines.

Water damage restoration in Coyote is shaped by the creek and by winter. A storm that raises the creek, a culvert that clogs or a roof that leaks can all produce calls. Our 24/7 dispatch line at (844) 950-6900 is the first step in each case.

Winter, the creek and the February 2017 lesson

On February 21, 2017, Anderson Reservoir spilled for the first time since 2006, and a levee on the Coyote Canal north of Cochrane Road failed and flooded northbound US-101. About 14,000 people were evacuated in San Jose, including neighborhoods such as Rock Springs and Olinder. The event showed how quickly water from this watershed reaches homes downstream.

If you live near the creek, look up your parcel on the FEMA flood map and line up sandbags before storm season. The City of Morgan Hill lists pickup sites on its website.

When water is spreading in Coyote

Call the 24/7 line the moment water enters the house or garage. Emergency water removal is available around the clock in Coyote, and during storms crews are sent in order of need. Tell the dispatcher how deep the water is and whether it came from the creek, a street or a pipe.

Creek water counts as contaminated, so do not wade through it with the power on. Our flood damage cleanup page explains what is removed and what can be cleaned, and storm water damage covers roof and window leaks.

If water is still spreading near the creek: prep steps

Prepare in this order when the creek is high or a storm is hitting.

Get to higher ground

Move people and pets to the highest room if water is entering at the ground floor.

Shut the main and the breakers

Close the main water valve. Switch off power only from a dry spot, and call the utility if the panel is wet.

Raise valuables

Put documents, electronics and keepsakes on shelves above the waterline, and photograph the waterline before cleanup.

Skip the shop vac

Household vacs can spread contaminated water and create shock hazards in wet rooms.

Places that anchor the community

Old stage road history

The Twelve Mile House stagecoach station once stood here, and the Coyote Grange Hall still marks the community center. Older properties nearby may have older plumbing, so a burst line can spread through long runs of aged pipe.

Rural lots along the creek

Many parcels are large and sit close to Coyote Creek. Drainage around the foundation matters, and outbuildings may be wet even when the house stays dry.
