Water Damage Restoration in Madrone, Morgan Hill, CA

Madrone mixes homes with a business park on its north side. Our 24/7 line sends a crew from Morgan Hill whichever kind of property is wet.

Madrone is the district at the northern edge of Morgan Hill, annexed to the city in the late 1950s. It lies near Capriano Park and borders both Coyote and San Jose. Homes, small businesses and the Madrone Parkway business park all sit within a short drive of each other.

Water damage restoration in Madrone covers two different kinds of calls. Households deal with burst pipes, water heater failures and roof leaks. Businesses along the parkway deal with suites, storage and equipment rooms. Call (844) 950-6900 and a dispatcher takes the details.

Homes in Madrone

A leak inside a house starts with the main shutoff. Close it, switch off power only if you can reach the panel dry, and call. The crew can start at any hour and drives up Monterey Road from the Morgan Hill base.

Hard flooring and finished lower walls are common targets. Our hardwood floor water damage page explains why wet boards can cup within days and what makes drying possible. Water that stays in walls needs structural drying before any rebuild begins. Professional drying is judged against the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration.

Business park and commercial calls

Suites in a business park can share roofs, walls and fire sprinkler lines. A broken sprinkler head or a failed rooftop unit can affect several tenants at once. Storage rooms and server closets need special attention because wet equipment and cardboard damage fast.

If you manage a property or lease a suite, call dispatch first and your property manager second. Our commercial water damage page explains how a business job is scoped, priced and documented.

Madrone borders South San Jose, and the line between cities can change who handles permits. Check your parcel's jurisdiction before major rebuild work.

If water is still spreading: before we arrive

These steps help whether you are in a house or a suite.

Close the main or the zone valve

Homeowners use the main. Tenants may need a building shutoff, so ask the property manager.

Switch off power to wet areas

Only do this from a dry spot. If the panel is wet, call the utility.

Lift and protect

Move small items, boxes and rugs off the floor. Raise furniture legs onto blocks.

Photograph first

Take photos of the source and the damage before cleanup begins.

Call dispatch

Call (844) 950-6900 with the address, the source and the rooms or suites affected.

Insurance steps after a loss

Report a sudden loss to your insurer promptly and keep receipts for emergency steps. Take photos of damaged items before they are moved. Coverage depends on your policy, and we do not decide it.

We give your insurer moisture readings, photos and equipment logs. The California Department of Insurance consumer hotline is 1-800-927-4357 if you have questions about your rights.
