Water Damage Service Area: Morgan Hill and Nearby Towns

One 24/7 line covers Morgan Hill and the places around it. Here is where our crews work and how each area differs.

Call (844) 950-6900

Morgan Hill and its ZIP codes

Morgan Hill, in Santa Clara County, has a population of 44,478 at the 2023 Census estimate. We serve the city across ZIP codes 95037 and 95038, from downtown along Monterey Road to the Madrone district in the north and the foothill lanes toward Paradise Valley.

Within the city we cross Downtown Morgan Hill, Madrone, Tennant, Paradise Valley and Llagas. A nearby landmark such as the Caltrain station or the Gavilan College campus helps dispatch find your address quickly.

Nearby towns with their own pages

Each nearby place has a page that explains its water and building context.

San Martin, ZIP 95046

A rural, unincorporated community south of Morgan Hill where wells and septic systems are common. Read the San Martin page.

Coyote, ZIP 95013

A creek-side community between Morgan Hill's Madrone district and San Jose. Read the Coyote page.

South San Jose, ZIPs 95123, 95111, 95136, 95138

A large city with many water suppliers and building types. Read the San Jose page.

Other places we take calls from

Calls also come in from Gilroy, Hollister, Watsonville, Freedom and Mount Hamilton. None of these has its own page, so ring the 24/7 line and dispatch will say whether a crew can reach your address.

How coverage works

Coverage is by address, not by ZIP code alone. Some ZIP codes cross town lines, and one, 95013 in Coyote, is a post office box ZIP. Tell dispatch your street address and cross street.

Crews leave from Morgan Hill. San Martin is roughly five miles southeast, Coyote roughly seven miles northwest and South San Jose roughly 22 miles northwest by road. Travel time varies with traffic and weather, and we do not promise arrival times.

Services across the whole area

Every service on our site is available across the area, and water work follows ANSI/IICRC S500 everywhere we go. The most requested are flood damage cleanup, water extraction and commercial water damage. The full list is on our services page.

Neighborhoods inside Morgan Hill

Downtown and Monterey Road

The original townsite, laid out in 1892, follows Monterey Road and Main Avenue. Our Downtown Morgan Hill page covers the mixed shops, restaurants and apartments here.

Madrone and the north side

Annexed in the late 1950s, this district sits toward Coyote and San Jose. See our Madrone page for homes and business parks there.

Tennant and Llagas

The Tennant area grew around the 21-Mile House stagecoach stop, and Llagas Road and Watsonville Road follow creek country that has flooded.

Paradise Valley and the foothills

Homes near Chesbro Lake sit in the foothills west of town. Hillside homes on Lake Anderson are covered on our Holiday Lake Estates page.

Creeks, floods and where to check your risk

Roughly 8 percent of Morgan Hill sits in a FEMA high-risk flood zone, and nine named creeks cross the city. The full creek list is on our flood damage cleanup page.

Llagas Creek has flooded six times since 2000, per Valley Water. To learn whether your own address is mapped, look it up on the FEMA flood map. The city posts sandbag pickup sites before storms, so check its website early.

Roads our crews use

Crews reach most of the area on Monterey Road and US-101. Cochrane Road, Main Avenue, Hale Avenue, Llagas Road and Watsonville Road cover the cross-town trips. When creeks rise, some of those roads flood, and dispatch will tell you if a route is affected.

If you are not sure how to describe your location, give the nearest cross street and any landmark you can see, such as a school, a park or a recreation center.

Water suppliers and who to call about the main

City water is pumped from two aquifers, Coyote and Llagas. Staff at the Edes Court yard keep weekday hours only, so at night a plumber is often the quickest way to learn which side of the meter holds the problem.

San Martin is different, because many homes there rely on private wells or small water systems that our San Martin page names. In South San Jose the supplier changes by neighborhood, and our San Jose page names the main one.

Permit offices across the area

Permit rules depend on where the address sits. The City of Morgan Hill's Building and Fire Prevention office, at 17575 Peak Avenue, handles city addresses. San Jose's Permit Center is at 200 E. Santa Clara Street, and unincorporated places such as San Martin fall under Santa Clara County.

Painting, wallpapering, carpeting and similar finish work are exempt in Morgan Hill, but rebuilding walls, wiring or plumbing often is not. Ask the right office before you plan the rebuild.
