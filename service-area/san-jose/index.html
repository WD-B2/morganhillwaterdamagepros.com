If water is still spreading: steps before we arrive

Use this order in a condo, townhouse or house.

Find the shutoff

Close the unit or main valve. In a condo, ask the building manager for the riser shutoff.

Mind the electrical

Turn off breakers to wet rooms only if the panel is dry. Wet outlets and fixtures call for a utility or 911 call first.

Save what is portable

Put electronics, documents and shoes on counters or beds. Lift rug corners to let air reach the floor.

Keep clear of dirty water

Water that smells of sewage or has a gray color should be treated as contaminated. Keep everyone out.

Call and notify

Call our 24/7 line, then your landlord, HOA or insurer.
