Hardwood Floor Water Damage Repair in Morgan Hill, CA

Wood swells within days of getting wet. We dry boards in place when we can and replace only the sections that will not recover.

Call (844) 950-6900All services

Hardwood floor water damage in Morgan Hill shows up as cupping, crowning, staining or boards that lift at the seams. Wood absorbs moisture from below and above, and it reacts within days. Call (844) 950-6900 and we will schedule a crew to measure and dry the floor.

If water is on the floor, mop it up and lift rugs and furniture. Run air conditioning at a cool setting and open windows only if outdoor air is dry. Do not seal the wet floor with polyurethane or cover it with rugs.

What water does to wood floors

Solid hardwood swells as it takes on moisture. Boards at the edges cup, with the sides rising higher than the center. If the top surface dries faster than the bottom, the boards crown instead. Both are signs that moisture sits below the surface.

Engineered wood and laminate behave differently. Laminate has a fiberboard core that swells and cannot recover. Engineered wood tolerates a bit more but can delaminate between layers. Our kitchen water damage team sees this beneath dishwashers often.

How hardwood is dried

Technicians measure the moisture content of boards and the subfloor. Injection drying systems push warm, dry air under the floor through small ports, or mats with suction pull moisture out through the surface. Dehumidifiers keep the air dry throughout.

Drying can take days or longer, depending on the species and thickness. ANSI/IICRC S500 guidance asks for documented moisture readings, not a fixed number of days. Floors that dry fast often flatten on their own. Floors that stayed wet for a week or more are less likely to recover.

Sanding, refinishing or replacing

Once boards flatten and moisture readings return to normal, a floor refinisher can sand and refinish. Wait until the wood has fully acclimated, since finishing too early locks in problems.

Boards that remain cupped or split are replaced. Matching an old finish can be tricky in a house with long-aged wood, so plan for blending across a room or hall.

Where wood floor damage starts in Morgan Hill homes

A burst supply line, a dishwasher leak or a slow slab leak are the usual causes. A leak under a slab can wick up into a wood floor over weeks. If you suspect that, read about slab leak water damage.

Hard water leaves a white film on wet floors as they dry. The city's 2021 report lists average hardness near 302 mg/L, so expect mineral residue to be part of the cleanup after a clean-water leak.

What to do on the first day

Remove rugs, mats and furniture legs that touch the wet floor. Rugs trap water and stain the finish within hours. Slide felt pads or foil under furniture legs that must stay put.

Wipe up standing water with towels and keep indoor air cool and dry. Avoid steam cleaners, wax and heat guns, which push moisture deeper or damage the finish. Take photos of the floor before anything changes, since cupping gets worse over several days.

Do not nail or screw down lifting boards. Fasteners trap moisture and can crack boards as they try to flatten during drying.
