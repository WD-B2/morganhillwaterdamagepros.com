Emergency Frozen Pipe Water Damage in Morgan Hill, CA

The damage often starts when the ice melts. Call our 24/7 line the moment a split pipe begins to leak or a frozen line starts to thaw.

Call (844) 950-6900All services

A frozen pipe in Morgan Hill can become an emergency without warning. The pipe may split when ice forms, but the water only pours out when the ice melts. Our dispatch line answers 24/7, so call at any hour once you see a leak or hear water running.

If a pipe is frozen but not yet leaking, shut off the main valve first. Then open the faucet it feeds and warm the pipe slowly with a hair dryer on low. Never use a torch or open flame.

When a split pipe does release water, close the main, move valuables off the floor and call. We take over the extraction and drying while a plumber repairs the line.

How often pipes freeze here

We do not quote freeze-day counts on this page because we could not verify a figure for the valley floor. The National Weather Service office for the Bay Area posts local forecasts and freeze warnings, and checking it before a cold night is the right habit.

What we can say is how the mechanism works. Cold air has to reach the pipe, and the water inside has to sit still. Pipes in unheated garages, attics, exterior walls and outdoor hose bibs are the ones that fail when a cold night does arrive.

Foothill lots and homes in Paradise Valley sit away from the warmer valley floor, so exposed lines there deserve a closer look on a clear, still night.

Frozen pipe emergency: what to do while it is still frozen

A pipe that is frozen but intact gives you a short window. Use it.

  1. Close the main valve. This limits the water that can escape if the pipe is already cracked.
  2. Open the nearest tap. Melting ice needs somewhere to go, and an open tap relieves pressure.
  3. Warm the pipe gently. Use a hair dryer, a heating pad or warm towels, starting nearest the tap.
  4. Look for wet spots. A bulge, frost line or damp drywall means the pipe may already be split.
  5. Call if water appears. Dispatch answers 24/7 and a plumber handles the repair.

Where split pipes cause the most damage

A split pipe hidden in an exterior wall can run for hours behind closed drywall. The first sign is often a stain at the baseboard or a patch of bubbling paint. By then the insulation inside has absorbed a lot of water.

In a garage or attic, a thawing line can drop water through a ceiling into the rooms below. Our burst pipe water damage team uses the same steps for those losses, since the water behaves the same once the pipe has opened.

Drying a wall after a freeze split

We open the wall at the wet area, extract what we can and set air movers to push dry air through the cavity. Dehumidifiers pull the moisture out of the air so the framing and insulation can release what they hold.

Moisture readings are logged each visit, and equipment stays until the wood reads dry. Frames dried this way rarely need replacement, which keeps repairs smaller. This follows the drying principles in ANSI/IICRC S500. Our structural drying page explains the equipment in more detail.

Frozen pipe repair cost factors

Cost depends on what the thaw reached. Five things set the price.

  • How many hours the split pipe ran before the main was closed.
  • Which materials got wet, with insulation and carpet pad adding removal work.
  • How many rooms or floors the water traveled through.
  • Days of drying equipment on site.
  • The rebuild scope for drywall, paint and flooring.

Preventing a repeat

Insulate lines in the garage and attic with foam sleeves. Disconnect garden hoses and cover outdoor spigots before cold nights. Leave cabinet doors open under sinks on exterior walls.

If you travel, keep the thermostat at a safe setting and ask someone to check the house. A vacant home with a split pipe can flood for days, and the result looks like a flood damage cleanup job.
